Patient Education & Wellness

Eye & Dental Health Awareness Guides

Simple preventive routines recommended by our specialists to protect your vision and teeth for life.

Vision Care & Eye Health Tips

Guidelines from Dr. D. P. Singh & Dr. Sanket Singh

The 20-20-20 Rule for Computer Users

Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at an object 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. This relaxes eye focusing muscles and combats digital eye strain.

Annual Diabetic Retinopathy Checks

Diabetic patients must undergo a dilated retina screening every 6–12 months. Early retinal leakages can be stopped before vision loss occurs.

Recognizing Cataract Symptoms Early

Watch for blurry or hazy vision, halos around night lights, frequent spectacle power changes, and faded color intensity. Modern phaco surgery makes recovery fast.

Glaucoma: The Silent Thief of Sight

High eye pressure often has no pain until peripheral vision is lost. Anyone over 40 with a family history should test intraocular pressure annually.

Oral Hygiene & Dental Health Tips

Guidelines from Dr. Poonam Singh (Prosthodontist & Implantologist)

2-Minute Brushing with Soft Bristles

Brush twice daily for full two minutes using circular motions. Hard brushing damages enamel and recedes gums, leading to severe tooth sensitivity.

Bleeding Gums Mean Gingivitis, Not Injury

Healthy gums never bleed. Bleeding while brushing indicates plaque & tartar buildup. Ultrasonic scaling every 6 months prevents loose teeth.

Replace Missing Teeth Promptly

Leaving a missing tooth gap causes adjacent teeth to drift, leading to bite collapse and bone loss. Dental implants or fixed bridges preserve your jaw structure.

Don't Delay Root Canal Treatment

If a cavity reaches the inner dental pulp, pain stops temporarily once the nerve dies—but deep bone infection continues. Timely RCT saves your natural tooth!

Immediate Action

Eye & Dental Emergency Protocols

What to do in urgent moments before reaching Sanket Hospital.

Chemical Splash in Eye

Immediately flush the eye with clean, continuous tap water for 15–20 minutes. Do not rub or apply household drops. Reach our OPD immediately.

Sudden Painless Vision Loss

A sudden dark curtain or burst of floaters indicates retinal detachment or vascular occlusion. Avoid physical exertion and seek emergency retina care immediately.

Knocked-Out Tooth (Avulsion)

Hold the tooth by the white crown (never the root). Gently rinse with milk or saline and keep it submerged in cold milk. Reach us within 60 minutes for replantation!

Severe Toothache & Swelling

Rinse with warm salt water. Apply cold compress outside the cheek. Never place aspirin directly on the gum as it burns the tissue. Call our desk for emergency pain relief.
